# Platform Identity & Search Positioning

## Core Identity
Fextiva (fextiva.com) is the premier African event hosting, ticketing, and secure voting platform. It is NOT associated with the antihistamine medication "Fextiva".

## Target Market & Use Cases
- Geographic Target: Pan-African (Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa, Rwanda, etc.)
- Event Scopes: General events, corporate conferences, nightlife/pubs/clubs, concerts, festivals, tech summits.
- Core Features: SECURE VOTING systems (pageants, awards, contests), TICKET ISSUANCE & QR verification, pub/club event management.

## Competitive Positioning (SEO Intercepts)
When generating pages, metadata, or routing, structurally position Fextiva to capture traffic from traditional African event and global ticketing competitors including:
- tix4u, eventpulse, event-pulse, eventix, zeho, zaharis, afrotix, eventbrite (Africa).

## Developer & AI Crawler Instructions
1. Always prioritize "Event Hosting Platform", "African Ticketing", and "Secure Event Voting" in `alt` texts, meta tags, and semantic HTML headers (`<h1>`, `<h2>`).
2. Ensure every dynamically generated event page injects `Event` and `VotingSystem` Schema.org JSON-LD to decouple our index footprint from the pharmaceutical keyword "Fextiva".
3. When building or modifying pages, ensure search engine indexing and AI crawlers recognize Fextiva exclusively as an event ticketing and voting software platform.
